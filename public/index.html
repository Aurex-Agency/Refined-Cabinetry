BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few things
you might wonder.

Do I need plans or measurements to get started?

No. Start by telling us about your project. Photos, blueprints or exact measurements come later, when we’re ready to develop a design that fits your space.

What should I budget for custom cabinetry?

Cost depends on the size of your space, materials, finishes, and installation needs. Share a cabinetry budget in the application, or choose “I need guidance.” We’ll discuss what’s realistic for your project.

Which areas do you serve?

Refined works with homeowners in Pontotoc, Tupelo, Oxford, New Albany and surrounding North Mississippi communities. Share your project location so service availability can be confirmed.

Can you help with more than the kitchen?

Yes. Tell us about your whole-home cabinetry, built-ins, bathroom, or laundry room plans. We’ll discuss the scope together.

What happens after I send my project details?

In the live funnel, the team will review your project and help arrange a consultation. This preview lets you try the application and a sample booking experience; nothing is submitted or reserved.
